# Projects

*Updated 14 October 2026*

## Current

### [Knowledge-Based Management and Information Leadership in the Emergency Services](https://www.pelastusopisto.fi/tutkimus-ja-kehitys/hankkeet/pelastustoimen-tietojohtaminen-koulutus-arviointi-ja-kehittamishanke/)

Project manager, along researcher I. Toth and research assistant K. Ruutala.

-   Evaluating KBM maturity across the Finnish emergency services
-   Recognizing key improvements for strengthening KBM processes and education
-   Developing basic and advanced educational material for fire-fighter, sub-officer, and officer education

### [Investigation of the National Siren System](www.pelastusopisto.fi/tutkimus-ja-kehitys/hankkeet/selvitys-kiintean-vaestohalytinjarjestelman-valtakunnallisesta-ratkaisusta/)

Project manager, along researcher M. Räkköläinen and research assistants L. Tolonen and S. Uronen.

-   Evaluating the current state of the regionally managed air raid siren system
-   Investigating and calculating national expenses related to the VIRVE 2 communications network implementation
-   Recognizing key stakeholders in production and logistics chains
-   Piloting acoustic simulations as planning and evaluation methods for system implementation

## Past

### [Determining the service needs of emergency services at the regional welfare counties](https://intermin.fi/hankkeet/hankesivu?tunnus=SM013:00/2025)

Principal investigator.

-   System-level analysis of financial and operational structures in Finnish emergency services
-   Critical appraisal of knowledge-based management processes
-   Suggestions for strengthening connections between resource allocation and operational behaviour on national level

### [Emergency Services Zero Tolerance to Discrimination and Harassment - Fact or Fiction?](https://pelastusopisto.fi/nollatoleranssi)

Principal investigator, along research assistants I. Silfverhuth and K. Huovinen.

-   Design and implementation of national survey study on discrimination, harassment, inappropriate behaviour, and violence in the emergency services
-   Suggestions for improving work-place health and well-being
-   Psychometric development of novel survey measurement tool for measuring inappropriate and violent experiences in emergency and rescue services
